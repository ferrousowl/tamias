Tamias

An AI treasurer for a small business on Arc. It decides when to pay, how much, and when to ask a human, and it writes down why. It runs inside a contract that enforces the owner's budgets, so it cannot spend past them. Every decision below was read from the chain on this page, and the hash chain linking the decisions was checked here too.
